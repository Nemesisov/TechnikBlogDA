Gaming Genres Explained

Shooter, RPG or something cozy? Every genre plays differently. Find out what makes each one special and which one fits you.

Ever wondered why you can play one game for hundreds of hours, while another one bores you after ten minutes? Most of the time, the reason is the genre.

What is a genre?

A genre describes how you play a game, not where its story takes place. A game set in space can be a shooter, a strategy game or a racing game. And it works the other way around too: two games can look completely different and still feel the same to play.

Find your genre

Shooter

You use ranged weapons and fight your enemies from a distance.

Examples: Call of Duty, Valorant

RPG

You play a character that grows in strength and experience over the course of the game.

Examples: The Witcher 3, Skyrim

Action-Adventure

Exploration, simple puzzles and combat come together in one journey.

Examples: Zelda, God of War

Sports

You play real sports with their real rules. Some games also let you coach and manage your own team.

Examples: EA Sports FC, NBA 2K

Horror

These games focus on fear and try to scare you with a dark atmosphere.

Examples: Resident Evil, Phasmophobia

Strategy

Careful thinking and planning lead you to victory. You collect resources, build a base and outsmart your opponent.

Examples: Age of Empires, Civilization

Survival

You start with almost nothing, collect resources and craft tools and shelter. The goal is to survive as long as possible.

Examples: Minecraft, Rust

Simulation

You control virtual lives, like a family, a farm or a whole city.

Examples: The Sims, Stardew Valley

Did you know?

  1. Minecraft is the best-selling video game of all time, with more than 300 million copies sold.

  2. RPGs go back to the tabletop game Dungeons & Dragons from 1974, long before they became video games.

Most games are a mix

Most big games today are not just one genre. They take the best parts of several genres and put them together:

  • GTA: Driving, shooting and story missions in a huge open world.
  • Fortnite: A shooter where you also build walls and ramps to protect yourself.
  • ARC Raiders: Shooting mixed with looting and survival. You collect gear and try to get out alive.

That is why it helps to know the basic genres. Once you know them, you can quickly tell what a new game has to offer.
