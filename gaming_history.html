Gaming History: Fifty Years of Pixels, Polygons and Pure Hype

It started with two white bars and a bouncing dot. Today, millions of players are counting down to Grand Theft Auto VI. Join us on a journey through gaming history.

Did you know that Nintendo is older than video games, or that Atari buried unsold cartridges in the desert? Our short video shares 13 surprising facts from 1889 to today, from the first "Tennis for Two" experiment to the PlayStation's secret Nintendo origins and a game industry that is bigger than Hollywood. Press play, and then read on for the full story behind the milestones.

Gaming history is older than most people think, but the first home console arrived in 1972. In 1972, the Magnavox Odyssey became the first home console, and the arcade hit Pong brought video games to bars and shopping malls. Only five years later, the Atari 2600 put interchangeable game cartridges into living rooms around the world. But the young industry grew too fast: in 1983, a flood of low-quality games led to the video game crash in North America. Many believed the hype was over. Then Nintendo arrived.

Since then, every generation has changed the way we play:

  • The 8-bit era (1980s): The NES and Super Mario Bros. rescued the market and created characters that are still famous today. The Game Boy followed in 1989 and made gaming portable.
  • The 16-bit era (1990s): The Super Nintendo and the Sega Mega Drive fought the famous console war, with faster action, better sound and unforgettable soundtracks.
  • The 3D era (mid-1990s to 2000s): The PlayStation brought 3D worlds and CD-ROMs into the mainstream. Its successor, the PlayStation 2, became the best-selling console of all time.

With the new millennium, gaming went online. The Xbox and Xbox Live made multiplayer part of everyday life, the Wii got whole families off the couch, and smartphones turned everyone into a gamer. Open-world games grew bigger every year, and no series symbolizes this better than Grand Theft Auto. GTA V, released in 2013, has sold well over 200 million copies and is still played today. Now the next chapter is about to begin: Grand Theft Auto VI is scheduled to launch on November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. After two delays and more than a decade of waiting, the return to the Vice City-inspired state of Leonida is one of the most anticipated releases ever. Whether it lives up to the hype, we will know soon. One thing is certain: it will write the next page of gaming history.

Pro tips

  1. Start with the classics: games like Super Mario Bros., Tetris and Pong are short, easy to learn and show you where modern game design comes from. Useful link: many of them are available in modern collections.

  2. Visit a retro gaming museum or event. Playing on original hardware feels completely different from emulators, and you will quickly see how far technology has come.
